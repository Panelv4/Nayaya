# Amendment impact — BNS s.107(1) — the penalty for abetting suicide rises from ten years to twelve

`amend.bns.107-penalty` · **REAL** · commenced 2024-07-01

IPC s.306 punished abetting suicide with imprisonment up to ten years. BNS s.107(1) re-enacts the offence with a maximum of twelve years. Nothing about what counts as the offence changed, so this is a pure sentencing amendment: it tests whether the machinery can see a change that moves no verdict at all. Section 107(2) (vulnerable victims) is new in the BNS and has no IPC equivalent, so it is identical before and after.
