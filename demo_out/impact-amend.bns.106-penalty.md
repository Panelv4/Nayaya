# Amendment impact — BNS s.106(1) — the penalty for causing death by negligence rises from two years to five

`amend.bns.106-penalty` · **REAL** · commenced 2024-07-01

IPC s.304A punished causing death by a rash or negligent act with imprisonment up to two years. BNS s.106(1) re-enacts the offence with a maximum of five years. Nothing about what counts as the offence changed, so this is a pure sentencing amendment: it tests whether the machinery can see a change that moves no verdict at all. Section 106(2) (dangerous driving, ten years) is new in the BNS and has no IPC equivalent, so it is identical before and after.
