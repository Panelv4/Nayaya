# Amendment impact — Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, 2019 — the penalty hike in s.185

`amend.mv.2019-penalty` · **REAL** · commenced 2019-09-01

The 2019 amendment raised the fine for a first offence under s.185 from Rs 2,000 to Rs 10,000 and for a second or subsequent offence from Rs 3,000 to Rs 15,000. The custodial ceilings were untouched: six months for a first offence, two years thereafter. Nothing in the offence itself changed, so this is a pure sentencing amendment and it tests whether the machinery can see a change that moves no verdict at all.
