How it works

Four steps from your account to a transparent, auditable response.

1

Create an account & login

Register with your name and email, then log in. You may then complete DigiLocker identity verification — a consent-scoped request that returns a hash-sealed identity artefact. It is optional: unverified reports are accepted and marked as unverified.

2

Explain your case

Write what happened in your own words, then confirm a few plain-language details (dates, type of case, your situation). The engine response is generated from exactly what you provide — nothing is assumed.

3

Attach your proof, if any

Photos, screenshots, documents or audio. Every file is stored and registered with a SHA-256 hash so the evidence set cannot be quietly altered later. No proof yet? You can confirm that explicitly.

4

Receive the response

See the reference ID, review level (L0–L3), urgency, severity tier, which safeguards fired and what happens next. All of it is deterministic, explained, and auditable. Was something wrong? Correct your answers and re-evaluate, and tell us with one tap — / feeds the improvement dataset.

5

Private until disclosed

Your case is visible only to you and administrators. You can request disclosure; it becomes public in the registry only after an administrator manually approves it — anonymized or full, never with account identities.

Narrative-first intake

The old technical “Runtime variables (JSON)” section has been removed. Victims now simply explain the case; the server derives the few internal values the engine needs from your plain-language answers. No JSON, no legal jargon.

Evidence register & fact mapping Live

Attached proof is hash-stamped (SHA-256) and listed in the case record. Phase 3 adds evidence-to-fact mapping: each file is linked to the case facts it supports or contradicts, with a coverage score showing how well the account of events is evidenced.

Strict admin-gated access Enforced

No one can view another person's case. Cases stay private until the owner requests disclosure and an administrator manually approves it (anonymized or full). Admin consoles, the technical workbench, raw evaluation and the audit log are administrator-only.

Legal knowledge base Phase 2

A versioned statutory database (only human-verified versions feed evaluations), precedent tracking, conflict detection, amendment-impact analysis and a full legal provenance graph — every citation, revision and disclosure decision is an auditable edge.

Feedback & re-evaluation Live

One-tap verdict feedback per case, bound to its revision; net-negative cases are auto-flagged for admin review. Corrections create new revisions with mandatory reasons — history is immutable — and every verdict is recorded in a log an administrator can export and audit.

DigiLocker verification Calibrated model

The full consent flow is implemented: scoped consent request with a PKCE challenge, a simulated DigiLocker consent screen, single-use authorisation code, issuer allow-list, artefact validation and a hash-sealed, expiring identity artefact that can be independently checked. The issuer response is generated locally and watermarked simulated.

Transparent routing Deterministic

Rules-as-code decides the review level, urgency and severity tier — with the exact triggers that fired, open questions for the judicial officer, and an immutable-style audit log entry per report.

Scope & honesty notice. NYAYA-RAC is a research prototype and decision-support tool. It is not a judge, adjudicator or sentencing authority, and it never decides guilt, innocence, sentence, compensation, bail or credibility. Its output is advisory and always requires human review at the computed level. Accounts and files are stored locally on this demo server with demo-grade security — do not upload real sensitive case material here. Statutory citations must be verified against an official corpus. Filing a false report is an offence; optional DigiLocker identity verification records a verified or unverified state with each report and helps deter duplicate and malicious filings.
