What this is, and what it is not. The statutes here are encoded by hand from the text on India Code. The packs are not yet reviewed by a lawyer — each one says so in its own status line. A passing analysis means the encoding is internally consistent and reproduces the illustrations printed in the statute. It does not mean the encoded law is right. One carefully verified provision is worth more than fifty seeded ones.
